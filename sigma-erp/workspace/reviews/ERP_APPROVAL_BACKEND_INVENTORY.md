## Owner rule used for classification

- Financial document: approval is the only point that may post accounting; Void flags the document and its owned journal without reversal.
- Nonfinancial document with no operational effect: approval/rejection or cancellation may apply; accounting Void does not.
- Nonfinancial document with operational effect: any cancellation needs its own guarded operational contract, not a journal rule. The owner decided on 2026-10-21 that GRN and ItemIssue stock quantities become effective at Approve; this inventory's Save-timing statements describe the still-unmodified source.

This inventory identifies drift against that rule. It does not freeze missing transition, permission, dependency, or migration contracts.

## Cross-cutting confirmed findings

1. Approval support is inconsistent: some aggregates have status only, some status plus decision log, and others no approval state.
2. `SaveJVAsync` is used by JournalVoucher, Receipt, CreditNote, DebitNote, CashDeposit, and CardTransactionBankTransfer, which supports a draft-before-approval shape but still creates a financial child before approval. Whether the owner permits an unposted child before approval remains a contract question.
3. `SaveAndPostJVAsync` is still called directly by CustomerDeposit and SalesAgreement workflows; these are clear candidates for “effect only on Approve” remediation.
4. `UnApprove` remains exposed for JournalVoucher and Invoice. The new terminal owner direction requires explicit reconciliation rather than silently retaining it.
5. Operational documents such as GRN, ItemIssue, StockTransfer, and Job cannot safely inherit financial Void. Their approval/cancellation must guard stock, job, billing, and conversion dependencies.
6. Existing legacy rows require nullable approval or an explicit backfill classification. Treating historical rows as pending would incorrectly authorize new effects.

## Unknowns requiring focused follow-up

- Whether standalone Payment rows are intended to become approved financial documents or remain source/representation rows owned by CashDeposit, payroll, bill-installment, or another aggregate; the current code supports multiple owners.
- Required accounting treatment for PDC receivable resubmission after a posted bounce; current source clears ownership links but supplies no balancing/void action.
- Whether invoice families share one aggregate lifecycle or require separate approval by subtype/source.
- Exact inventory posting transaction, reservation, historical-row classification and cancellation behavior for GRN/ItemIssue; transfer balance integration remains unproven.
- Whether Job approval gates only status or also reservations, issues, purchasing, time, and invoicing.
- Required approval-step policy: current evidence supports single manual decisions for several documents, but no global multi-step settings policy was proven.
- Permission names, notification behavior, and actor/date fields for the future universal approval surface.

## Migration planning boundary

No migration was created. A future implementation should group migrations by established aggregate contracts, not create one speculative universal migration. At minimum, aggregates without status/log require nullable legacy-safe status, append-only decision history, concurrency protection, and owned-effect linkage where absent. Financial aggregates also need a durable owned journal reference and terminal Void metadata; operational aggregates need durable movement/effect ownership instead.
