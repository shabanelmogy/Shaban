## 1. Financial ownership and accepted boundaries

Used rental fleet is the primary sale case; trade stock remains a distinct existing cost source. Preparation transfers a physically returned/free explicitly identified financial asset into sale inventory at verified base-currency carrying value. It is not a sale invoice, a customer receipt or an automatic new rental. Transfer is effective through its date inclusive, final depreciation prorated by actual calendar days in that month; later depreciation stops. All expected positive prior monthly depreciation must have effective posted nonvoided coverage. Missing/duplicate/voided/later or historically unidentifiable source accounting is rejected with a specific reconciliation message, never guessed from a chassis string or last report snapshot. Base-currency-only guards reuse the current financial engine; no invented FX conversion. Generalized cross-currency disposal is not claimed supported.

New operational financial sources remain direct. No hard dated booking/reservation policy changes. Existing same-source locks/order are retained and extended: sale -> depreciation for transfer; sale -> journal allocation for invoice/settlement/closure. A depreciation writer never acquires the sale lock in reverse order. All dependent document/JV/state writes share one existing guarded transaction.
