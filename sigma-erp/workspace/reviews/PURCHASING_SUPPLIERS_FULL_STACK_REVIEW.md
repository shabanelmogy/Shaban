F1 detail: a saved and approved PO with no Bill is correctly NotBilled; this was overstated in the initial discussion and is not a bug. The current Bill Add contract contains `GoodsReceivedNoteIds` and BillDetail contains `GoodsReceivedNoteId`, but neither contains a direct `PurchaseOrderId` (`BillAddVm.cs`, `BillDetailAddVM.cs`). The Purchase Order list displays and filters the persisted binary BillStatus, yet its current UI does not call `toggle-bill`. Thus a GRN-backed Bill can be traced to a PO, while a direct General/Job Bill cannot reliably be tied back by a printed number or supplier. The business definition of "Billed" should say whether it means any linked draft, any approved Bill, or full approved coverage; a partial state may be needed for multiple GRNs.
