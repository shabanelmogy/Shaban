Agreement creation reserves selected active available Equipment with model/chassis and no open equipment-rental delivery; quotation creation additionally enforces model quantities/prices and converts approved/unexpired quotation. The editor inherits quotation customer, prices, down payment, installment count and terms; it sends a complete item snapshot and serial/date/cheque/bank schedule overrides. Direct contracts allow their own customer/prices/discount/terms. Required positive outstanding creates installments; fully prepaid agreements use zero installments. Planned down payment does not count as collected.

Invoice is Open-only and date >= agreement date. It revalidates reserved/active units, current model/chassis, no rental, customer, every positive PurchaseCost, revenue/tax/cost/stock/AR account and open period. It creates Sales MiscellaneousInvoice and posted JV (Dr customer total; Cr sales net; Cr VAT; Dr cost and Cr stock per unit), marks agreement Invoiced and units Sold/inactive. Delivery is Invoiced-only, posted live invoice required, delivery date >= invoice date; creates one separately numbered sales delivery note per unit with receiver/notes, marks agreement/units Delivered. Delivery has no full-payment gate. Closed requires Delivered and outstanding <=0 on server (detail clamps outstanding to0); UI Close shown at outstanding0.

Received totals include every nondeleted/nonvoided direct agreement receipt plus linked quotation receipt, independent of receipt approval. Allocation: down payment first, then installments by date and numeric serial. Due report keeps allocation over entire agreement even outside selected date range; SQL filters/count/totals/sort/page and only selected page materializes. Report includes live Open/Invoiced/Delivered/Closed agreements, excludes Cancelled/deleted, and unpaid only unless includePaid. Plate column represents the first agreement unit, not a per-unit installment.

Cancel: Open-only and no nonvoided/nondeleted receipt with SalesAgreementId. Quotation-only receipts still count toward paid/financial edit lock but are intentionally outside frozen D7 direct-receipt cancel gate. Cancel keeps history, releases Reserved units and returns Converted quotation to CustomerApproved. Delete applies same direct-receipt gate then generic usage checks: even a still-live voided direct receipt can remain an inbound reference and block deletion. No automated refund, post-invoice cancellation, credit-note conversion or restoration to stock is promised. Nonvoided direct or quotation receipt locks financial edits (agreement date/first date/customer/units/prices/discount/downPayment/count); schedule dates/cheques/banks, documents and permitted terms remain editable while Open. Quotation terms remain source-owned.

Receipt reversal/edit/delete after Closed can reopen to Delivered when outstanding appears, via ReceiptService frozen cross-service lock; unvoid rechecks collection eligibility and limits. This service was inspected via baseline/directly related source facts but not edited in this workstream. Client walkthrough sections Agreement–Close/example were checked with no factual mismatch reported.
