Bill now owns nullable ApprovalStatus and append-only BillApprovalLog. New entity initializer is NotApproved; EF has no default/backfill that marks old rows approved. Explicit old-record reset records a real new decision and requires source ownership; an ambiguous/non-source-owned old JV is not silently unposted. Source-owned unposted JV identity survives reset/edit and is rebuilt only on explicit reapproval. Financial snapshot validation occurs detached before any resolver that may save the shared context. Approve/reset/Remove serialize with allocations under allocation→PurchaseRequest tenant locks, and state is an EF concurrency token with conflict handling. No journal approval is presented as Bill approval.

Zero-total approved Bills have no new posting; reset from a formerly positive Bill may retain the old unposted JV identity. Its stale draft lines have no ledger effect and are rebuilt on later positive approval. Existing receipt reservation and loan calculations remain; saved tax snapshots must still match, not silently change at approval. Finance terms alone create no cash/payment/interest entry.

## Allocation contract and capacity audit

GET Bill/{id}/allocation returns only BillId/No/DueAmount/MoneyDecimalPlaces and sources SourceDetailId/No/Date/Description/AvailableAmount. POST receives only unique source-detail identities and amounts; journal/account/partner identities and balances are derived afresh. Four named DTOs each have their own file. Controller, interface, partial service, Angular interfaces/service and dialog agree on names, nullability and routes.

Sources are live same-tenant posted/nonvoided supplier debit journal lines on the payable account/effective branch/currency, excluding the target JV. Null generated currency normalizes to the configured base-currency identity so explicit-base manual JVs are eligible; real currency mismatches are not converted. Only existing ledger capacity is linked. No client amount creates a cash movement or updates LoanEMI.Paid.

Both sides subtract effective explicit-line allocations. Legacy source-JV reservations without a line are applied once in stable order across their same-supplier/account debit pool. Unsupported historical Bill-as-source header-only records disable new allocation rather than expose potentially consumed capacity; their old displayed status is not guessed/repaired. Positive representable, precision-valid amounts must fit each source and the cumulative target due under both locks before a single allocation save. Safe failure preserves the draft; explicit Refresh Sources confirms discarding changed rows, performs GET only and never retries a mutation automatically.

The focused independent allocation audit confirmed ownership, explicit/legacy capacity and shared lock order. It identified the null/explicit base-currency mismatch (corrected) and uncertain target-header-only historical shape (failed closed). Root additionally traced DebitNote source transitions: their existing Add/Update/Remove/approval/void lacked the allocation lock. Direct locking closure now serializes those supplier source writers too; their Serializable multi-save boundary is preserved. Bulk Add/Update previously bypassed domain cores through inherited raw mapping, so overrides now invoke the same protected cores within one owned Serializable transaction. Business calculations/transitions/messages were not otherwise refactored; legacy raw error-message handling remains outside this concurrency correction.
