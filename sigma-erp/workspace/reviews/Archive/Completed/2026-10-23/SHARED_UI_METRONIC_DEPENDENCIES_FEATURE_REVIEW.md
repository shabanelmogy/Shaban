## Findings and recommendations

Closure applies to the source-read-only review, not implementation or production acceptance. Findings below remain migration work.

### Confirmed outcome

Keep the existing shared presentation layer. PrimeNG is already the table/dialog/control implementation behind much of it. Do not replace shared wrappers with direct feature-owned PrimeNG markup, duplicate shared components, or force every native element into a PrimeNG widget. A shared owner may be a component, directive, service, global token or bounded style rule. Existing consumers inherit a shared change; feature-owned copies/overrides require their own adoption review and cannot be claimed automatically uniform.
