How to manage Error Logs

How to manage Error Logs?

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Error Logs module.

Select a date range and click on the REFRESH button.

All error logs will appear on the screen as a list. You can export this data to excel.
