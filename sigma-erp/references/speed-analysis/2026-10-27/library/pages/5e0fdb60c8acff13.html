INCREASE YOUR INSURANCE ACCESS REVENUE & DECREASE YOUR VEHICLE REPAIR COST

In paper-based vehicle checkout & checkin inspection, you can forget to charge the customer for any chargeable damage & it becomes your cost to repair the damage. Speed – Vehicle Leasing System (VLS) mobile app provides you facility to do vehicle checkout & checkin inspection through mobile. You can mark damages & take multiple pictures of each damage.

You get the customer electronic signature on mobile app for the acknowledgement. System will automatically charge the customer for any damage which is not covered. You can upload accident report through mobile app & web app.

If customer is at fault & he/she has not take sufficient waiver, the system will charge automatically. Digitalization of damage charging process minimizes customer disputes & speed up the debt collection of your damage invoices.
