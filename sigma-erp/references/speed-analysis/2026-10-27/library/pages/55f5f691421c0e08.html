Downloading Speed – UK Vehicle Safety Check System (SCS) Android Mobile App

  1. Open Google Play Store on your Android mobile phone or tablet.
  2. Type in the search box “UK Vehicle Safety Check System”
  3. You will find the “UK Vehicle Safety Check System “ mobile app. Install it.
  4. You can download Speed – UK Vehicle Safety Check System Android Mobile App by clicking the link https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.speedautosystems.ukscs&hl=en
