Rental Agreement Editing

Go to the rental module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the tab stating “Agreements”.
All the opened and closed agreement can be found here.
1. Click on the small blue button with the settings sign on it and there will be many options.
2. Click on the Edit option from there and you can edit the agreement.

Note: Once a agreement is closed then there is no option for edit.
