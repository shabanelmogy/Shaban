How to define and manage Roles

How to define and manage Roles?

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Roles module.

A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+CREATE NEW ROLE” button.

Click on the SAVE button.

Click on the ACTIONS button if you want to edit a Role.

Click HERE if you have a query or an issue.
