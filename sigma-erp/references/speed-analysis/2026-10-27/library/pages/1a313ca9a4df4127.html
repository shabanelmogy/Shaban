Fleet Utilization Optimization

Fleet utilization is crucial for measuring sales effectiveness. It’s calculated based on the ratio of idle vehicles to those currently rented out.

For example, if you have 1000 cars in your fleet and 900 are rented out at a given time, your fleet utilization rate would be: Fleet Utilization Rate = 900 / 1000 = 90%

Optimizing fleet utilization means minimizing idle vehicles and maximizing rentals, leading to increased sales and revenue. This optimization is essential for every car rental business.
