Rental Agreement Opening

  1. Click the “Rental” link in the left navigation panel
  2. Click “Agreements”
  1. Agreement will be shown on right panel
  2. Click button “+Open Agreement” on right top corner to open a new rental agreement
  1. Select the agreement type (daily, weekly, monthly) from “Type” field
  1. Enter data in compulsory fields. Save button will not enable till all compulsory fields are filled.
