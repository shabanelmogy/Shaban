Speed Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS)

If you are a leasing operator with different types of supplementary transportation services like rental, chauffeur, limo, passenger transportation & trying to find some comprehensive software solution then you have come to the right place. We provide a comprehensive solution Speed Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) which not only provide the automation of leasing operations but also the automation of all your supplementary transportation services. Further we provide the automation of your Finance Department, Sales and HR Department. Benefit of a single platform provides you strong visibility into your business, revenue optimization and cost minimization.

If your whole operation is leasing without any other supplementary transportation models, Speed Vehicle Leasing System (VLS) will still be very beneficial for you due to automation of all your manual processes like Traffic Fines Process Automation, Road Tolls Process Automation, Fuel Process Automation, Damage Charging Process Automation, Vehicle Inspection Process Automation using Mobile App etc.

Lease Process Automation

Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) fully automates the Leasing Process. It can be changed as per your specific leasing requirements. Default Leasing Process Automation is as per below steps:

  • Lease inquiry handling
  • Lease quotation preparation
  • Lease quotation internal approval
  • Lease quotation revision as per customer request
  • Lease quotation customer approval
  • Master Lease agreement preparation
  • Individual Lease agreement preparation
  • Temporary vehicles delivery if required 
  • Lease vehicles procurement
  • Lease vehicles preparation
  • Lease vehicles delivery
  • Lease invoicing
  • Fines, Tolls, Excess Mileage, Damage Invoicing
  • Early Lease Termination Handling if there is.
  • Lease Agreement Closure
  • Lease Agreement Performance Analysis

Mobility Solution

Field processes automation which were not cost effective in the past are now feasible due to low cost of mobile internet & low cost of mobile devices. Further, mobile connectivity coverage is almost everywhere. Speed Mobility Solution automates all your remote leasing processes like:

  • Vehicle Inspection using Mobile App on:
    • Lease Vehicle Delivery
    • Lease Replacement
    • Lease Vehicle Collection
    • Non-Revenue Movement Opening
    • Non-Revenue Movement Closing
    • Workshop Movement Opening
    • Workshop Movement Closing
  • Customer digital signature on Mobile App for the acknowledgment of vehicle checkout & check-in
  • Drivers Live Tracking on Google Map
  • Drivers Tasks Management to optimize Drivers’ Performance
  • Drivers Rating by customers while getting signature from customer on Mobile App

Manual Processes Automation

Each process optimization & automation improves your profitability. Each manual process is a burden to maintain. Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) provides the automation of almost all manual processes like:

  • Traffic Fines Process Automation
  • Road Tolls Process Automation
  • Fuel Process Automation
  • Payment Process Automation

If you want to automate any manual process specific to your business or specific to your country, we can definitely work together to automate it.

Documents Management System

It is a cumbersome job to file all the physical documents related to leasing operations. Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) has its inbuilt Document Management System (DMS).

  • Vehicle Documents:
    • Vehicle Registration Card
    • Vehicle Insurance Policy 
  • Corporate Customer Documents:
    • Trade License
    • Credit Approval Documents
    • Chamber of Commerce Certificate 
  • Individual Customer Documents:
    • National ID/Passport
    • Driving License

    Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) has full electronic file of Lease agreement containing:

    • Signed Lease Agreement
    • Lessee Documents
    • Vehicle Lease Delivery Document
    • Vehicle Replacement Vouchers
    • Vehicle Check-out & Check-in Documents

Email & SMS Notification Engine

Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) has a Notification Engine to notify customers, suppliers & employees for different notifications. Notification can be email or SMS or both depending on the type of notification.

Different types of automatic notification are:

  • Invoice emailing on invoice generation 
  • Statement of Account emailing on month’s end 
  • Overdue invoice/debt reminder email/SMS
  • Vehicle Registration Expiry alert email/SMS
  • Vehicle Service Due Reminder email/SMS
  • Traffic Fine alert email/SMS
  • Credit Card Expiry email/SMS
  • Driving License Expiry email/SMS
  • National ID/Passport Expiry email/SMS
  • Sales Promotion email/SMS
  • Agreement email
  • Receipt email
  • Vehicle Check Card Email on Vehicle Mobile Inspection

Lease Performance Analysis

Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) provides you Lease Performance Analysis from different angles like: 

  • Profit per Individual Lease Agreement
  • Profit per Master Lease Agreement
  • Forecasted (Budgeted) Profit vs Actual Profit
  • Profit per each Lease Customer

We are working to provide you more insights into your lease performance.

Add-on Systems

As per your business requirements, you can add any additional add-on systems available on Speed Software Platform.

Being part of the Speed Platform, each system can talk to other systems without any need of integration. Data flows seamlessly from one system to other system. Different available add-on options are:

  • Car Rental System (CRS)
  • Limo System (Limo)
  • Transportation System (TS)
  • Workshop System (WS)
  • Financial System (FS)
  • Human Resource Management System (HRM)
  • Customer Relationship Management System (CRM)

Interface with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft or Sage

Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) can be interfaced with any known ERP like SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Epicor or Sage using one of three methodologies:

  • We provide you our system API & your ERP implementer build the interface using our API
  • We share the data in staging database which your ERP implementer use to build the interface 
  • We build the interface by using your ERP API

Multilingual

To operate the software in a language which is not yours; can add more complexity in already existing complexed business. Further for your domestic regulatory compliance, you may have no option except to generate all legal documents related to Leasing operation into your native language.

Speed  – Vehicle Leasing Software has the facility to operate it in your language. If you don’t like some label or caption; you can change it through the Language Settings Layer.

Accessible from Anywhere

Speed – Vehicle Leasing Software (VLS) can be accessed from anywhere using any device laptop, mobile, MacBook, tablet etc. You just need browser & internet connection.

On a bigger display, you will use software in full screen mode while on a mobile screen, the system will automatically change in mobile mode. This accessibility convenience really improves productivity which may cause improvement in profitability.
