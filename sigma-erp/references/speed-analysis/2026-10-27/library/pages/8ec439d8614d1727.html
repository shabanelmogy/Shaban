Paperless Car Rental

Storing documents like customer passports, driving licenses, and national IDs in physical files or computer folders can be time-consuming and cumbersome. Retrieving these documents when needed can be a hassle, and sometimes they can even be misplaced. Speed Car Rental Software (CRS) solves this problem by allowing you to store all these important documents electronically. You can access them anytime, anywhere, making your car rental business paperless and environmentally friendly.

 

Electronic Rental Agreement with Electronic Signature

With Speed Car Rental Software (CRS) Mobile App, you can easily create and open rental agreements, then collect customer signatures directly on your mobile device. The system will automatically email the electronic rental agreement to both you and the customer. You can print the agreement at any time from the software. Additionally, if you’re using the Speed CRS web app, you can connect a signature pad to capture customer signatures electronically. This process helps reduce or completely eliminate paper usage in your office, making operations more efficient and eco-friendly.

 

Passport & Driving License Electronic Reading

At busy rental locations, such as airport counters, manually entering passport and driving license details can be time-consuming and prone to errors. Speed Car Rental Software offers an efficient solution by allowing you to electronically capture passport and driving license information using specialized scanners. The system automatically reads and inputs all the relevant details from the documents. It also takes digital copies (pictures) of the passport and driving license, storing them securely in the system for easy access and reference. This feature significantly speeds up the check-in process and reduces manual data entry.

 

Car Rental Brokers Integration

Speed Car Rental Software offers seamless integration with car rental brokers, making the booking process more efficient. Whether through a simple XML import or full-scale API integration, you can easily sync bookings from brokers directly into the system. This eliminates the need for manual data entry, saving time and reducing the potential for errors, while enhancing overall operational efficiency.
