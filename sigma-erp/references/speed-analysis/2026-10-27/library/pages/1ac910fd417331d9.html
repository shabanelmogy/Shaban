Speed Human Resource Management
HR is frequently concerned with time-consuming administrative activities, manual processes, and the requirement for quick decision-making. Speed Human Resource Management Software (HRMS) responds to these difficulties by offering Automotive HR Solutions.
  • Product: Speed Human Resource Management (HRM)
    • Masters Areas, Branches, Departments, Divisions, Grades, Allowances, Designations, Banks, Shifts, Asset Types, Passes Types, Skill Types, Jobs Types, Memo Types, Minimum number of Employees for one Department to be used in Leave Planner, Minimum number of Employees for one Division to be used in Leave Planner, Leave Types along with payment structure, Leave Assigning Authorities
    • Employees Management
      1. Basic Info: Employee Code, Name English, Name Arabic, Father Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Nationality, Email Address, Contact Number Local, Address Local, Contact Number Overseas, Address Overseas, Blood Group, Attendance Machine Code, Employee Photograph (Image Attachment)
      2. Employment Info: Department, Division, Branch, Designation, Joining Date, Agreement Date, Visa Status, Labor Contract Type, Working Hours, Over Time Eligibility, Ticket Eligibility with period, Basic Salary, Allowances
      3. Skills Record, Qualification Record, Training Record, Bank Accounts, Nomination
    • Definitions of different types of Employee Documents to be managed by system like passport, labor card, labor contract etc
    • Employees Documents Management along with scanned images
    • Definition of different types of Branch Documents to be managed by system like Trade License, Tenancy Agreement etc
    • Branch Documents Management along with scanned images
    • Company Assets Stock Management
    • Allocation of Company Assets & passes to Staff

      1. Employees Documents Expiry (Passport, Visa, Labor Card, Health Card etc)

      3. Company Assets & Passes Expiry (Vehicles Registration & Insurance etc)
