Manage Master Agreements

Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Lease tab.

Scroll down and click on the Master Agreements module.

Here you can see all agreements. For searching a particular agreement, put the details like master agreement number, status, customer, date range, and/or quotation reference number, and then click on REFRESH button.

All Agreements will appear as a list in the window.

You can view details or take print by clicking on the ACTIONS button.

You can also export the data to excel by clicking on the EXPORT TO EXCEL button.
