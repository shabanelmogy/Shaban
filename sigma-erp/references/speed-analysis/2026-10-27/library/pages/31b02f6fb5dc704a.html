Rental Agreement Deposit Management

While opening an agreement right under the advance amount there is a section for Deposit as well.
You have to enter the deposit amount here. This amount can be used for fines or tolls or can also be adjusted for the due amount.
We can charge this amount throught cash or other means as well.
For deposits management you have to open Vouchers from the left navigation panel.
There you will find the option for Deposits open that and you will see all the deposits taken from the customers.
Click on the action button with the deposit for more options related to that deposit.
You can use this deposit if needed. It can be refunded or extend or can also be transefred to other agreement with same customer.
