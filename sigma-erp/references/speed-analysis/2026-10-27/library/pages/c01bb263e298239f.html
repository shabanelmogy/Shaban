How to Repair Vehicle Damages in Speed – VLS

How to Repair Vehicle Damages in Speed – VLS

Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.

Scroll down and click on the Repair Vehicle Damage module.

Put the Plate No. in the search bar and click on the SEARCH button.

Diagram of that vehicle will appear with damages marked on it.

Remove the marks that you have got repaired.
