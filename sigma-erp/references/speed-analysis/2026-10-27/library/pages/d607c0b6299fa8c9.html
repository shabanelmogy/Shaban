Vehicle Replacement on a rental agreement

Vehicle Replacement On A Rental Agreement

Go to the Rental module from the left navigation panel and open the agreements options.
Here you can see action button with every agreement.
click on that action button and a drop down menu will come select the replace vehicle option.
When you click on it a new window will pop up.
You have to enter the details of the replacement vehicle and press save and by this you can simply replace the vehicle.

Note: Replacement can only be made on open agreements.
