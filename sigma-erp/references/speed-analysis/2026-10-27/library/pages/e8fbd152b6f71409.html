How to define monthly Invoicing and Lease Invoicing

How to define monthly Invoicing and Lease Invoicing?

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Settings module.

Go the Common tab.

Scroll a little down and you will see the heading of Monthly Invoicing and Lease Invoicing. Adjust figures as per your business and fleet requirements.

Scroll down and click on the SAVE button at the end of the dashboard.
