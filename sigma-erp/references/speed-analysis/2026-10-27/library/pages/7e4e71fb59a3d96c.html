UK Vehicle Safety Check System (SCS) – Features

Before getting started, it would be better to get enough know-how of the App as a stitch on time saves nine. UK Vehicle Safety Check System (SCS) is easier and stress-free.

Though salient features are listed below, yet we are always open to going for customizable solutions. So, you can ask to add, modify, or remove any feature as per your needs.

Dashboard

The dashboard appears as soon as you open the App. So, it is the main interface and is further divided into seven default modules. These seven default modules are;

1.      Fleet
2.      Inspections
3.      Administration
4.      Support
5.      Data Entry Corrections
6.      Customizations
7.      Subscriptions & Billing

1.      Fleet

The Fleet module allows you to add, edit, and remove vehicles as quickly and easily as you need. It usually deals with the vehicles’ details, management, and current status. A typical “Fleet” module does hold further three options.

. Vehicles

Under the “Vehicles” option, you would be able to add a new vehicle by clicking on the “Create New Vehicle” tab. Also, you can see a list of all the added vehicles in certain a hierarchal fashion. Moreover, it will allow you to sort out vehicles based on the status.

. Vehicle Types

Here you can add and manage vehicle types. By clicking on “Create New Vehicle Type” you will see a comprehensive form to add details and save. These added vehicle types would help you to assign a certain type to a vehicle when adding a new vehicle in the above-mentioned vehicles module.

. Vehicle QR Labels Inspections

Once you have added all the details and type, you can generate distinctive QR labels for each vehicle. Customizable sizes of QR labels will enable you to paste them either on key-chain or the door of your vehicle.

2.      Inspection

As the name suggests, this module will enlist all the performed inspections in one place. Moreover, you will be able to choose dynamically between date range and vehicle to see what inspections were carried out. For instance, select a date range like 15/05/2020 – 20/05/2020, click on the “Search” tab and get the list of all the vehicles inspected in the meantime.

3.      Administration

The administration module bestows full control over the management of the SCS App. So, go like a professional and grasp like a boss. The administration panel is also divided into further options.

. Branches

UK Vehicle Safety Check System (SCS) is a one-stop-solution. So, it offers you to manage as many branches as you want with this single system. Branches module allows you to add your business branches located at other territories, cities, and even in other countries.

. Safety Check Templates

The safety check process requires different things to inspect and record as per the vehicle type. For instance, what required for a PSV is different than the HGV does. So, to record the observations, you need different templates. Speed – UK Safety Check System (SCS) App offers three default templates that you can use initially.

. Roles

Roles option enables you to create a certain role with specified permissions. So, you are in full control to create a role and apply limitations or authorities.

. Users

Users tab lets you manage all the “users” that are logged in to your system. For instance, you have a manager to perform a daily walk-around vehicle check, you would be able to add him, assigning the role of safety manager from Roles tab, to allow him to use the App effectively.

. Languages

You can choose the language as per your region and native inclination. In this option there a dropdown list of languages of which you can select anyone. Is your language not listed? No problem, you can ask us to add it.

Automatic Form Template As Per DVSA Regulations

Inspection forms are integrated into the Speed – Safety Check System (SCS). These forms are formulated according to the UK’s Driver and Vehicle Safety Agency (DVSA) requirements. So, you can record every essential observation that the authorities demand.

Inspection Templates According To The Vehicle Type

Either you have an HGV, PSV, or a personal Van, Speed – Safety Check System (SCS) provides you with the customized templates. So, the form for HGV inspection would include all the details required for HGV inspection, and so do for PSV and Personal Van.

All Inspections On Web App

Once you have done your inspection with the smartphone App, all the inspection data would be available on the Web App. So, after executing a vehicle’s walk-around check, you have to go for Web App which you can open in any browser either on your smartphone or PC. On the Web App, get Signed-In and have all the inspection data ready to treat in either way.
