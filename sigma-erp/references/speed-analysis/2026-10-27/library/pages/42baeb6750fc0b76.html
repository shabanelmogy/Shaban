Billing Settings of Company Account

Open the companies module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the second tab for Companies.
Click on the blue button stating “Create New Company”
A pop up will appear and go to the last tab from top stating “Billing Info”.
1. Here you have to define Credit Period of company that how much long the credit account can be hold.
2. You have to select the limit for Company credit in the Credit Limit.
3. YOu can add the company in Tax Return Number in TRN.
4. Enter the credit card details if the company provides.
