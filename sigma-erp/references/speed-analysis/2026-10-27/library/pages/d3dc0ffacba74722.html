How to adjust VAT settings

How to adjust VAT settings?

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Settings module.

Go the Common tab.

Scroll a little down and here you will see slots for VAT Percentage and VAT Inclusive and Is VAT Optional. Adjust them accordingly.

Scroll down and click on the SAVE button at the end of the dashboard.
