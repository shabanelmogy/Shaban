How to manage Divisions module

How to manage Divisions module?

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Divisions module.

A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+CREATE NEW DIVISION” button.

Fill the form and click on the SAVE button.

If you want to Edit/Delete any DIVISON, click on the ACTIONS button and choose action accordingly.
