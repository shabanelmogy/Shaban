How to manage Service Log

Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.

Scroll down and click on the Service Log module.

Now, put all or few parameters like plate number, date range, workshop, or service item. Then, click on the REFRESH button.

A complete list of the service log will be displayed in the window. You can print or export this data by clicking on the REPORT button.
