Early Closing of Monthly Rental Setting

Early Closing of Monthly Rental Setting

These settings are related to the monthly invoicing rule.
You have to enter the days from 0 to 6 and if the agreement is closed within 6 days system will charge daily rate.
You have to enter days from 7 to 28 and if the agreement is closed before the month than it will be charged on weekly basis.
