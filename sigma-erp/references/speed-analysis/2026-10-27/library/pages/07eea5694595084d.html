Updating Vehicles

  1. Select the fleet module from the left navigation panel.
  2. Click on the fleet tab, and then on Vehicle.
  3. Here you will see the action button with every vehicle.
    When you click the action button a window pops up:
    a. You can view the details of vehicle.
    b. you can edit or update the vehicle.
    c. You can update the millage.
    d.You can also see the agreements history made on that specific vehicle.
