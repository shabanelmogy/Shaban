Speed Limo Software (Limo)

Speed Limo Software (Limo) is the best Limo Software for the automation of Limo Bookings, Dispatchment, Trips Tracking and Drivers Tracking. Speed Limo Software (Limo) consist of:

  1. Limo Software Web App
  2. Mobile App for Drivers
  3. Mobile App for Passengers
  4. Corporate Customers Web App

Speed Limo Software (Limo) enables you to streamline your Limo operation and make it smooth and speedy. Speed Limo Software (Limo) is a feature-rich and powerful system that let the operators manage their operations on the go through mobility based features.

How Speed Limo Software (Limo) Helps you?

  • No need to manually enter the location through location master, Speed Limo Software (Limo) comes with Google Maps Integration.

  • Driver are notified for the trips dispatchment, modification, cancellation through Driver Mobile App. Passengers can track their trip through mobile app.

  • Your corporate customers can book limo & view dispatched trips, trips in progress, completed trips through web app.

  • You can operate Speed Limo Software (Limo) in your own language.

  • You can record vehicle damages along with pictures whenever car is taken back from one driver & issue to next driver on duty.

  • Speed Limo Software (Limo) can be customized as per your specific business requirements.

  • Speed Financial Software (FS) is the part of Speed Software Platform & seamless integrated to Speed Limo Software (Limo). You can see real time financial impact on your financial books while running your limo operation.

  • The limo software effortlessly links with the workshop software, enabling seamless coordination between bookings and maintenance for a streamlined and efficient service.

Speed Limo Software (Limo) Features

  1. Limo Tariff based on Google Maps Areas & Locations.
  2. Limo Tariff based on:
  • Time
  • Distance
  • Hourly
  • Half Day
  • Full-Day
  • Point to Point Fixed Rate
  1. Corporate Limo Tariff
  2. Tariff for Tour Packages with multiple points for individuals & corporate customers
  3. Limo Bookings:
  • Corporate customers will be provided User id & password.
  • Corporate customers can book limo online
  • Bookings will automatically come to Speed Limo Software (Limo)
  1. Dispatcher can attach driver & car to any limo booking
  2. Driver will get notification for any new trip in the mobile app
  3. Driver will take customer from his/her location & press the “Start” trip button in mobile app
  4. Driver will press “Close” trip button to close the trip after reaching destination.
  5. Completed trip will be automatically updated in main Limo Software.
  6. Limo Invoicing (bulk invoicing at month end for corporate/credit customers)
  7. Limo Trips Reporting Engine:
  • Total trips for a particular period
  • Trips on a specific car
  • Trips on a specific customer
  • Trips on a specific driver
  • Un-invoice trips
  • Invoiced trips
