Why does Data Loss happen?

There are several reasons that may lead to data loss ranging from a human error to technical concerns. However, incompatibility among various solutions used in the same business is the major reason of data loss.

For instance, if you are using a fleet management software of different company and a finance solution from another, then there are increased chances of data loss due to faulty mapping.
