How to manage Cost Centers

How to manage Cost Centers?

Cost Centers module allows you to categorize your costs into smaller heads like retail cost or management cost, etc.

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on Cost Centers module.

A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+CREATE NEW COST CENTER” button.

Fill the form and click on the SAVE button.

If you want to Edit/Delete any cost, click on the Actions button and choose action accordingly.
