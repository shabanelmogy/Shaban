How to adjust charges for leasing agreements

Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.

Scroll down and click on the Settings module.

Go the Charges tab.

Select module either Rental or Leasing.

Some default options of charges will appear in a windows.

Click on the Add Row option given in the below left side of the charges list.

A new row will appear. Now, choose charges type by clicking on the arrow depicted in the row. Choose further parameter and click on the SAVE button.

You can change the order of the list by clicking and holding long on the grey area of a row. Drag the row and release it where you want to place.

To delete a row, click on the cross shown in red color tab in every row.
