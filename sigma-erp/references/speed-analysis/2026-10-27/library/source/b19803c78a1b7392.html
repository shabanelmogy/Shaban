E-Fines Traffic Fines Downloader for Vehicle Leasing Operators

Being operating lease company, you have workload to handle many traffic fines. E-Fines eliminate the workload on your staff to charge traffic fines. E-Fines will itself download the traffic fines from authority web portal. Downloaded traffic fines will be shared with your leasing system. Finally, your leasing system will charge to your lease customers. Whole process will require minimal human intervention.

Benefits:

  1. No human effort required to collect traffic fines data
  2. Automatic charging of traffic fines to your lease customers
  3. Email notification to your lease customer
  4. SMS notification to lease customer
  5. Traffic Fines payment record
