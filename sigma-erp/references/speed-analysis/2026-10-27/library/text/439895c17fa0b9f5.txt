How to change the Voucher number

How to change the Voucher number?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Change Voucher No. module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Select the voucher type, put the current voucher number, and new voucher number.
Click on the CHANGE button.
