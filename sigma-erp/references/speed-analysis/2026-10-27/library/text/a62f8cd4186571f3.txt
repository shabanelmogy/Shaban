Deleting Vehicles
Select the fleet module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the fleet tab, and then on Vehicles.
Here you will see the action button with every vehicle.
When you click the action button a window pops up and there is the last option is for deleting the vehicle from the fleet.
“If there are any aggrements made then system will not delete it you have to contact support for this”
