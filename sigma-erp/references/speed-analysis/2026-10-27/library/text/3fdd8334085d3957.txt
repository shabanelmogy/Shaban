Editing Company Customer Account
Open the customer module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the second tab for Companies.
Click on the blue button stating “Action” with the companies.
Here a drop down menu will pop up and select edit.
By clicking edit the details related to that company will appear in a new window and you can edit the company customer account from here.
