Create and manage Service Items
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.
Scroll down and click on the Service Items module.
Now, click on the +CREATE NEW SERVICE ITEM button.
A pop-up window will appear. Put the service item name and click on the SAVE button.
