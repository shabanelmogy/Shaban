How to create and manage fines
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Settings module.
Go the Fines tab.
Click on the +CREATE NEW TARIFF FILE button. And fill the details. Then, click on SAVE button.
Fines Account will appear enlisted in a window.
To view, edit, or delete a Fines Account click on the ACTIONS button.
