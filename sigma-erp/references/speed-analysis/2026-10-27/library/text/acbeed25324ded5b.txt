How to create Contact Groups?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Contact Groups module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Go to the “Type” and choose either Customer or Supplier.
Now, click on the “+” button given on the top-right side, fill the form, and click on the SAVE button.
