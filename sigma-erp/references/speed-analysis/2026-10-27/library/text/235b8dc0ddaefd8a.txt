How to create vehicle QR Labels?
This feature is only available on the web application so you need to use the web app available on https://app.speedautosystems.com/Account/Login so login on this link.
By clicking “Vehicle QR labels” you’ll have access to the feature allowing you to generate or print Vehicle(s) QR Labels in following ways:-
The individual vehicle by selecting a specific plate number and leaving Branch blank.
All vehicles of the company, by leaving plate no blank and selecting [All] in Branch.
All vehicles of a specific branch by selecting it in Branch and leaving Plate No blank.
QR Labels can be generated or printed in two formats or sizes e. for Car Door or Key Chain. These printed QR Codes can be pasted on the desired place in a vehicle and/or Key Chain and can subsequently be used for quick identification of the vehicle.
