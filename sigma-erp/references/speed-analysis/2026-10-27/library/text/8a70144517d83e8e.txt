Monthly Invoicing Rules Settings for Monthly Rental
Monthly Invoicing Rules Settings for Monthly Rental:
This option is related to the monthly invoicing that how do you want to charge your customer if the agreement is monthly basis.
Individual Customers : You can select the invoicing rule according to your company that how you charge your customers. You have to select from 3 invoicing rules. Second option is about the type that do you charge the customer in advance or charge them after the services.
Corporate Customers:
Same options as the individual customers you have to select the invoicing rule and the type.
