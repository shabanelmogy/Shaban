How to manage Vehicle QR Labels
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.
Scroll down and click on the Vehicle QR Labels module.
A window will open. Now, select plate number and branch name (if any).
Choose the type of QR label either Door Label or Keychain Label. Then, click on the GENERATE QR LABELS button.
Now, click on the PRINT QR LABELS button and get QR labels printed.
