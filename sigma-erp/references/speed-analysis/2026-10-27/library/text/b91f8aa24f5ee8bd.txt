Rental Agreement Advance Amount Handling
whenever you are opening a new agreement the system gives you a option for advance amount. This amount is taken in advance while openeing the agreement and can be used to settle down the due amount.
This option is available only while opening the agreement.
When you are opening the agreement fill in all the details and at the end you can see there is a box of advance and you have to enter the amount in it and press save.
