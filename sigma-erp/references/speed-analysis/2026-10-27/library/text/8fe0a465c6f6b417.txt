How to correct Vehicle Status?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Correct Vehicle Status module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Select a vehicle here.
Click on the CONVERT button.
