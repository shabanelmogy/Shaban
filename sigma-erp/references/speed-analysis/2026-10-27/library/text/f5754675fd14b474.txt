How to merge contacts?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Merge Contacts module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+SEARCH & ADD” button.
Fill the form and click on the OK button.
Now, select the required contacts and click on the MERGE CONTACTS button.
