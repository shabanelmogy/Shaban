How to create and manage Suppliers
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Customers tab.
Scroll down and click on the Suppliers module. A window will open.
Click on the +CREATE NEW SUPPLIER button.
A form will appear. Fill the details and click on the SAVE button.
Suppliers will appear in a listed format.
To view, edit, or delete, click on the ACTIONS button.
