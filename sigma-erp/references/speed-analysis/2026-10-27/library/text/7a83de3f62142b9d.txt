Uploading Company Documents
Open the customer module from the left navigation panel.
1. Click on the second tab for Companies.
2. Click on the blue button stating “Create New Company”
3. A pop up will appear and go to the next tab from top stating “Documents”.
You can enter the company Trade License or any such document here with the details and the picture as well.
