The lease module lets you manage all the lease processes.
How to define and adjust Tariff
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Lease tab.
Scroll down and click on the Tariff module.
Select a date range and click on the PROCESS button. No of processed fines in total and with agreements will appear.
You can download this data by clicking on the DOWNLOAD button integrated on the top-right side of the window.
