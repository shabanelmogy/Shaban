How to use Payable Age Analysis
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Customers tab.
Scroll down and click on the Payable Age Analysis module. A window will open.
Select a date range. Choose options of customer, customer group, customer type, or sales person, etc. then, click on the SEARCH button.
All the concerning data will be displayed.
To print click on the PRINT button. And to export the data click on the EXPORT button.
