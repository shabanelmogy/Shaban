How to manage holidays
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Settings module.
Go the Holidays tab.
Click on the +CREATE NEW HOLIDAY button. And fill the details. Then, click on SAVE button.
To view, edit, or delete a Holiday click on the ACTIONS button.
