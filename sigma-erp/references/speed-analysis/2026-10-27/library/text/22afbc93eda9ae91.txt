How to create an office branch in Speed – VLS?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Branches module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Now, go for the +CREATE NEW BRANCH button on the top right side of the dashboard, click on it.
A form will open. Fill the form with Branch Code, Branch Name, TRN (Tax Return Number), and State and click on the SAVE button.
A newly created branch will appear on the dashboard.
