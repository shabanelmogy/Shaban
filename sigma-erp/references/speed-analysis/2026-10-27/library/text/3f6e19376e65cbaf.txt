How to open vehicle movement on a staff?
To Check-Out a vehicle use the mobile application and check-out process is spread over six steps:-
Step#1: tap “NRM Check-Out” or “Staff Check-Out”.
Step#2: fill in the required information, like enter Vehicle Plate Number or just scan the QR code this will fetch the vehicle details automatica Enter driver’s name, enter mileage on the odometer of vehicle and fuel level and give the location from where the vehicle is being checked out. Keep the location of cell phone “On” so that system can record the geographical location automatically.
Step#3: tick all the items available with the vehicle and tap “Next”.
Step#4: check the vehicle for exterior damages if any and mark these and take a photo to keep the record by tapping the camera button, complete process is already explained before in Agreement Check-In Process.
Step#5: check the vehicle for interior damages if any following the steps explained in Step#4.
Step#6: Enter the notes if required, get driver’s signatures and tap “Finish” to finish the check-out process.
