Weekend Settings
Weekend Settings:
If your Company give special promotions or charge extra on weekends then add your weekend days accordingly.
If you select the option “Mix Weekend with normal rental” then there will be no effect in the system and its charges.
You can also set a limit of KM for the weekend. If no limit then leave it blank.
