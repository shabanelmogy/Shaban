Companies
Go to the Administration module and click on it.
Click on Data Import, a drop-down list will appear.
Click on the Companies module, a dashboard will open on the right side of the menu panel.
Choose an Excel file and click on IMPORT FROM EXCEL button.
Companies Data will be imported automatically.
You can also download Sample Companies Data by clicking on the DOWNLOAD SAMPLE FILE button.
