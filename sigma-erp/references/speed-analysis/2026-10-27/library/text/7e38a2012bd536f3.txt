Languages
The default language of the Speed – UK Vehicle Safety Check System (SCS) is English. However, if you are an expatriate living in the UK and want to operate the SCS App in your native language, then you can go to the language module and choose as per your choice. However, you can also ask us to add your language manually if your language is not enlisted there.
