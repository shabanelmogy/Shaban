How to manage individual customers
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Customers tab.
Scroll down and click on the Individuals module. A window will open.
Click on the +CREATE NEW INDIVIDUAL button.
A form will appear. Fill the details, add documents, and billing information. Then, click on the SAVE button.
Individual customer will appear in a listed format.
To view, edit, or delete, click on the ACTIONS button.
