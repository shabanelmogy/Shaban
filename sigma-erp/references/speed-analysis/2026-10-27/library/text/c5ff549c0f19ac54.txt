How to manage Users?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Users module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+CREATE NEW USER” button.
Click on the SAVE button.
Click on the ACTIONS button if you want to edit a User.
Click HERE if you have a query or an issue.
