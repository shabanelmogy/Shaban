New VEHICLE MOBILE INSPECTION App for Automotive Mobility
Speed Auto Systems is excited to launch a new version of VEHICLE MOBILE INSPECTION covering all the inspection needs of different automotive mobility. Vehicle mobile inspection (VMI) is ideal for car rentals, leasing operators, car dealers, workshops, fleet management, delivery services, and accident claim management companies. The Speed-vehicle inspection app provides seamless check-ins/outs management, recording every damaged detail on every trip. The vehicle mobile inspection app enables you to manage your vehicle logs digitally over your mobile, eliminating complex paper-based car inspection. Paperless car inspection will save time and increase your staff’s productivity.
Speed knows the challenges faced by companies in automotive mobility. Keeping track of vehicle conditions and maintaining accurate records manually can be a hectic task. That’s why we have developed an efficient App that simplifies and digitalizes the entire inspection process.
Silent features of VEHICLE MOBILE INSPECTION:
Seamless check-out and check-in management.
Accurate damage recording.
Effective Damage History record
Digital logs with cloud backup
Paperless Go Green approach
Digital Signatures Acknowledgement
Accessible anywhere from any device.
QR identification code.
