How to View/Edit/Delete a Contact Group?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Contact Groups module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Go to the “ACTIONS” button and click on it.
A drop-down list with View, Edit and Delete options will appear. Choose action accordingly.
