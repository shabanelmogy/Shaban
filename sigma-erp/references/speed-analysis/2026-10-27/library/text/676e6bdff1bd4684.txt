How to create a new Language?
If you didn’t find your language in the given list, then follow the below process.
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Languages module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the +CREATE NEW LANGUAGE button.
Choose language and flag. Then, click on the SAVE button.
