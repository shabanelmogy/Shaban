Rental Agreement Extension
Go to the Rental module from the left navigation panel and open the agreements options.
Here you can see action button with every agreement.
click on that action button and a drop down menu will come select the Extend option.
The same agreement will be opened in front of you and you can extend the agreement by changing its return date and press save.
