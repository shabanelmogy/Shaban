Currency Settings
Click on the Administration Tab on the left side Navigation panel and then click on Settings Tab.
Click on the Common tab and select Currency Code e.g AED for UAE Dirham, Currency Name e.g Dirham is Dubai Currency name, and Decimal Currency Name e.g Fil for Dirham.
