What if you have to only scan a code and all the information, specifications, and details of a certain vehicle appear on the screen? Wouldn’t it be amazing? Of course, it will make you feel more reliable and robust in front of your customers.
How do QR Codes work?
There are a few steps that you have to follow in order to get appropriate QR codes ready for every vehicle. These steps are mentioned below;
Grab the Speed CRS App
Enter all the details of your vehicle.
Go to the QR codes section
Click on the print QR code.
You can select either a key chain version or a door version.
Now, paste the printed QR codes either on keychain or vehicle doors.
Whenever you need details of that particular vehicle, you will have to just scan that code.
