Create and manage vehicle types
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.
Scroll down and click on the Vehicle Types module.
A window will open. Go the +CREATE NEW VEHICLE TYPE button and click on it.
A form will appear. Fill it and add specify diagrams. Then, click on the SAVE button. All vehicles will be listed with name and details.
You can also search here any vehicle by typing vehicle type. To view, edit, or delete, click on the ACTIONS button.
