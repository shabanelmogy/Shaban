How to download Vehicle Mobile Inspection for Car Rentals (VMI.Rental) app from Google Play Store?
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.speedautosystems.vmi.rental
To download and install mobile application go to Google Play Store for Android devices and in search box give VMI and press search. Select Vehicle Mobile Inspection (VMI) of Speed Auto Systems LLC, download and install this application.
This will appear in your mobile application Tap it to start and give your credentials to login if already signed up if not Tap “Sign Up”. Fill all the details and Click “Sign up” button.
