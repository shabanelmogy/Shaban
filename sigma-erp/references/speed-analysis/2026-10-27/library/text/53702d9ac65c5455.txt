Manage Vehicle Not Ready reasons
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Vehicle Not Ready Reasons module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Click on the “+CREATE NEW REASON” button.
Click on the SAVE button.
