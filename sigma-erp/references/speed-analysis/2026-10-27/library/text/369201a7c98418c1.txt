E-Payment System
Speed Auto Systems is proud to introduce our cutting-edge E-payment System, specifically designed for the automotive industry to automate Payment Collection Processes from Customers. We understand the challenges posed by manual payment collection ways and our goal is to provide you with a seamless and efficient solution.
Say goodbye to the manual task of requesting payments from customers. With our automatic e-payment system, invoices are sent directly to your customers’ email addresses. They can conveniently settle their payments by clicking the payment link provided on the invoice using the credentials Credit Card. Our electronic form eliminates the need for printed invoices, saving costs and reducing environmental impact. Also, reduces the need for manual follow-ups and ensures a hassle-free payment experience for your customers.
Silent Payments and Direct Debits are two distinct features of our E-Payments Automation System.
Our system activates silent payments, automatically processing payments in the background whenever they are due from the client’s side. With silent payments, you can automate the payment collection process effortlessly. When a payment is due from a customer, our system quietly identifies it and charges the customer’s credit card. This streamlines the payment process, saving your time and effort.This means you no longer have to manually track and request payments.
Direct Debits
Direct Debits facility offers a convenient way to collect car lease installments directly from your customers’ bank accounts. This feature provides flexibility and ease of payment for both, you and your customers. Automatically on the basis of a Pre Agreement Between the Account Holder, Bank, and Customer. The agreed-upon amount will be deducted automatically, ensuring a smooth and reliable payment process. Hence, Car Lease installment can be deducted easily from the bank.
