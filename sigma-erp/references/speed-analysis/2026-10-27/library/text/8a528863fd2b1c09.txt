Suppliers
Go to the Administration module and click on it.
Click on Data Import, a drop-down list will appear.
Click on the Suppliers module, a dashboard will open on the right side of the menu panel.
Choose an Excel file and click on IMPORT FROM EXCEL button.
Suppliers Data will be imported automatically.
You can also download sample Suppliers Data by clicking on the DOWNLOAD SAMPLE FILE button. Click HERE if you have a query or an issue.
