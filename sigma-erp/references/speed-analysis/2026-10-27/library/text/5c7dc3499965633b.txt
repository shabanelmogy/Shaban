Vehicles
When you have to know about your fleet, you would have to click on the vehicles tab. This tab will show all the vehicles enlisted categorically. On the top right side, you will see a button of “+ CREATE NEW VEHICLE.” This button allows you to add a new vehicle as per your demand and need. Moreover, you can search for a specific vehicle by adding “Plate No” or “Status.”
