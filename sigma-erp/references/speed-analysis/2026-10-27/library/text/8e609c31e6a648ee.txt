How to set Cost Parameters
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Lease tab.
Scroll down and click on the Cost Parameters module.
A window will be opened. Go to the top-right side and click on the +CREATE NEW PARAMETERS button.
A small window will pop-up. Choose the cost parameter name and order. Then click on the SAVE button.
That parameter will be enlisted in the window. You can view, edit, and remove by clicking on the ACTIONS button.
