How to define Cancel Cheque Payable Number?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Settings module.
Go the Document No’s tab.
Scroll down, locate, and adjust Cancel Cheque Payable Number.
Scroll down and click on the SAVE button.
