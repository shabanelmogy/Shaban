So, What Is Vehicle Profitability?
Every vehicle consumes a certain amount of funds when operating in the rental industry. These are the expenses that you have to bear unavoidably. So, you can make a profit only if the revenue generated by that vehicle is more than the expenses on it. This is called vehicle profitability and is counted in percentage. You can count vehicle profitability as;
Vehicle Profitability = Revenue – Expanses / Expanses x 100
For instance, a certain vehicle has earned $2000 on a specific day. Meanwhile, the overall expenses on that vehicle are $500, then the profitability would be;
Vehicle Profitability = $2000 – $500 / 500 x 100 = 300%
In order to calculate the vehicle profitability, you have to record, measure, and analyze the revenue as well as expenses of that vehicle.
