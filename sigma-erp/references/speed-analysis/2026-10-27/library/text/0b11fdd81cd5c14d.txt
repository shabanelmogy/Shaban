How to define Services
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Fleet tab.
Scroll down and click on the Service Items module.
Now, choose among three options; default, make wise, or model wise.
Click on the +CREATE NEW SERVICE button. You can also import data by clicking on the IMPORT button on top-right side of the window.
