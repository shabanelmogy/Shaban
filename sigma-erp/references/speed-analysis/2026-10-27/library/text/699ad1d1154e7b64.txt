Define and manage staff Designations
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Staff tab.
Scroll down and click on the Designations module. A window will open.
A window will open. Go the +CREATE NEW DESIGNATION button and click on it.
A new small window will pop-up. Write the designation name here and click on the SAVE button. New designation will be available in the list.
You can view, edit, or delete by clicking the ACTIONS button.
