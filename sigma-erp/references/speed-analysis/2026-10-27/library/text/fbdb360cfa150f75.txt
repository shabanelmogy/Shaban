How to select Languages?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Languages module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel with several languages enlisted.
Click on the ACTIONS button.
Click on the Set as a default language option.
