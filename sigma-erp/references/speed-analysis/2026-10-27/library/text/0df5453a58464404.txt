Rental Agreement Opening
Click the “Rental” link in the left navigation panel
Click “Agreements”
Agreement will be shown on right panel
Click button “+Open Agreement” on right top corner to open a new rental agreement
Select the agreement type (daily, weekly, monthly) from “Type” field
Enter data in compulsory fields. Save button will not enable till all compulsory fields are filled.
