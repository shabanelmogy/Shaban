Adding Vehicles
Select the fleet module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the vehicles tab.
In this window you will see a blue button on the right upper corner named “Create New Vehicle”.
When you press that button a window pop up will appear.
In this pop up you have to mention each and every detail of that specific car accordingly.
Plate no. is a unique as it cant be repeated and make and model of the vehicles are already entered just enter the initials and it will appear in the system.
