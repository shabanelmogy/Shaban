How to manage Audit Trails?
Go to the panel on left side, scroll down to Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on Audit Trails module.
Fill in the form with date range, record number, record type, and user, etc.
Click on the SEARCH button.
All the Audit Trail details will be opened. You can export this data to excel as well.
