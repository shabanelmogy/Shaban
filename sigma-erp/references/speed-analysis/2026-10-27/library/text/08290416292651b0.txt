Data Entry Corrections
Again if you have a Service Level Agreement (SLA), you can ask us for a data entry correction. At times, you may face any data entry related issue. If this happens, go to the Data Entry Corrections Module and click on the “+CREATE DATA ENTRY CORRECTION.” Our support team will contact you and help you to fix that issue.
