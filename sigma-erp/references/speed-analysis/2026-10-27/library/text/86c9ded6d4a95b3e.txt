How to adjust companies as customers
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Customers tab.
Scroll down and click on the Companies module. A window will open.
Click on the +CREATE NEW COMPANY button.
A form will appear. Fill the details, add documents, billing information, and drivers’ details. Then, click on the SAVE button.
A company will appear in a listed format.
To view, edit, or delete, click on the ACTIONS button.
