How to adjust the date, time, and time zone?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Settings module.
Go the Common tab.
Here you will see slots for Time Zone and Date Format. Adjust them accordingly.
Scroll down and click on the SAVE button in the end of the dashboard.
