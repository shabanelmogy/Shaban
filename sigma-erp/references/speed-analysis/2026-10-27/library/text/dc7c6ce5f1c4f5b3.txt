Individuals
Go to the Administration module and click on it.
Click on Data Import, a drop-down list will appear.
Click on the Individuals module, a dashboard will open on the right side of the menu panel.
Choose an Excel file and click on IMPORT FROM EXCEL button.
Individuals Data will be imported automatically.
You can also download sample Individuals Data by clicking on the DOWNLOAD SAMPLE FILE button.
