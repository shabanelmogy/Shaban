Vehicles QR Codes Generation
Select the fleet module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the Vehicle QR Label Tab.
Click on blue tab “Generate QR Labels” on the top right corner, QR labels will be generated.
You can generate a single QR label by only entering a plate no of vehicle.
System allows you to print QR labels in 2 sizes 1 for Door label and other one for KeyChain.
