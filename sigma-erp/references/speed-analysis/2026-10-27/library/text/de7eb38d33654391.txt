Uploading Individual Customer Documents
Open the customer module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the first tab for individual customers.
Click on the blue button stating “Create New Individual”
A pop up will appear and go to the next tab from top stating “Documents”.
Here you can add any type of documents for the customer. System allows you to upload a file in form of image so you can scan the document and upload it to the system.
