Vehicles Documents Management
Select the fleet module from the left navigation panel.
Click on the fleet tab, and then on Vehicles.
Click on “Create New Vehicle” on the upper right corner.
A popup window will appear , you will see a “Documents” button click on that button.
Here you can see a Blue Button on the right side. From this button you can upload the documents of specific vehicle.
