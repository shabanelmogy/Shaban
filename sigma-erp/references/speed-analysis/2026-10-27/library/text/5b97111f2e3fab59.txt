How to Edit/Delete an office branch in Speed – VLS?
Go to the panel on the left side, scroll down to the Administration module and click on it.
Scroll down and click on the Branches module.
A dashboard will appear on the right side of the panel. Now, go for the ACTIONS button (integrated with each branch name), and click on it.
A list with two options Edit/Delete will appear. Click on either option to perform an action.
