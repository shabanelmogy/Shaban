Define and manage Clauses of lease agreements
Go to the panel on the left side and click on the Lease tab.
Scroll down and click on the Clauses module.
Click on the +CREATE NEW CLAUSE TYPE button.
A new window will be opened. Specify a name and click on the SAVE button.
